What is MalangNyang Jump?

MalangNyang Jump is a casual jumping game where a cute cat hops through cloud-filled skies. Simple left and right taps move the cat, and each world has several stages with coins to collect for skins and trail effects.

  • ControlsOne-thumb play with left and right taps
  • WorldsAdventure, Jelly, Toy and Aurora Snowfield
  • CombosLand on clouds in a row to build combos and chase your best score
  • Dress up20 cat skins and 27 trail effects
