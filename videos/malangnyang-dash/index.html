What is MalangNyang Dash?

MalangNyang Dash is a 3D cat running game set on the road that leads out of a storybook. Ride with a cat across three lanes, gather coins, and race two rivals on the same road for the lead.

  • ControlsSwipe sideways to change lanes, up to jump, down to dash
  • Stages3 worlds (Adventure, Jelly, Toy) and 60 stages
  • Races3-cat races against two rivals — 1st place takes the top of the podium
  • Dress up20 cats, 12 rides and 24 trail effects
