About PangChu Studio

PangChu Studio (팡츄 스튜디오) is an independent game studio that develops PongNyang Jump and PongNyang Dash. It publishes animation and music on PongNyang and runs the dedicated kids animation channel PongNyang Kids.

PongNyang

PongNyang (퐁냥) is PangChu Studio’s series of cat games and animations. The game series was renamed from MalangNyang (말랑냥이) to PongNyang on October 24, 2026. Its official YouTube channels are PongNyang for animation and music and PongNyang Kids for children’s animation.

Games

PongNyang Jump
A casual game where a cat jumps from cloud to cloud.
PongNyang Dash
A 3D running game where a cat races across three lanes.
